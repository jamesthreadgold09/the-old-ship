# The Old Ship: project conventions

Guidance for anyone editing this site, including AI assistants. Keep the site fast, simple, and warm in tone.

## Writing style

Write like a person, not an AI. The voice is a friendly village free house: warm, plain, and to the point.

### No em dashes

Do not use em dashes (the long dash) in site copy. They are one of the clearest tells of AI-written text. Rephrase with the right punctuation or wording instead:

- a comma for a light pause
- a colon to introduce a list or an explanation
- parentheses or "such as" for an aside
- a full stop to split one long sentence into two

Only use an em dash if there is genuinely no clearer alternative. This should be rare, so treat it as the exception rather than a habit.

Keep en dashes (the short dash) for numeric and time ranges, for example 12–2:30pm or Monday–Thursday. These are correct and should stay.

This rule applies to all visible copy: section text, headings, buttons, captions, image alt text, the page title and the meta description.
